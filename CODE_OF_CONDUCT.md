# Code of conduct

Terminal Canvas follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, and keep criticism about the work rather than the person.
Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not welcome in issues, pull requests or anywhere
else this project lives.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour here worries you, contact the maintainer privately through the
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/alexnieves-cs) rather than in a public thread. Reports are
read by the maintainer only and handled in confidence. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject
comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may block someone
who keeps doing so.
