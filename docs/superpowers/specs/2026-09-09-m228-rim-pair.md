## What this milestone is

full from here.**

## The recipe, and why it is a token

`--rim-top: inset 0 1px 0 var(--rim)` is declared on **bare `:root`**, with the radii and the
durations. The *geometry* of a specular top edge — inset, 1px down, no blur, no spread — is
theme-invariant; only its colour is a theme's. Writing it once matters because it is restated at
**every** rule that writes the frame's `box-shadow`, and those rules already exist for a reason
that has bitten before: each of them restates `--lift`, or a selected panel drops to the ground
when clicked (M109's own comment says so). The rim inherits that hazard exactly, so it inherits
the same fix rather than a new one.

**It is not folded into `--lift`.** `shadow.1` reads `var(--lift)` in body rules by name and
counts its sites; a composite token that swallowed it would make the one resting shadow
unfindable by the check that exists to keep it the only one.

## The sites

| Surface | Wears | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `.panel` and its seven state/selection rules, `@keyframes wants-you-pulse` | `--rim-top` | the frame is the raised surface; every rule that restates `--lift` restates the rim with it |
| `.launcher` | `--rim-top` | it wears the frame (`shadow.1` already names it beside `.panel`) |
| `.panel__slot` | `--rim-inner` | the terminal well |
| `.launcher__well` | `--rim-inner` | it is called a well and it is one |

**`.panel__slot` may take no padding, no border and no margin** — the block above it in
`styles.css` says why in full: `xterm-pointer.ts` re-expresses a click against the SLOT's rect
while xterm subtracts `.xterm-screen`'s, so any inset between the two survives as
`d * (1 - 1/scale)` and every click at a zoomed-out scale lands a column or more off the cell the
user aimed at.

**A `box-shadow` changes no layout box.** That is the whole reason this milestone may touch that
rule at all — the same property M144 relied on when it made the chrome's counter-scale a
transform. The well reads as cut into the frame without one pixel of xterm's geometry moving,
and the terminal keeps painting `--well`: **the recess is the FRAME's, never the cells'.**
