# M340 — verify:visual is a signal again: `shot.cjs`'s ledger arguments, and the golden debt re-judged

**Verdict: shipped.** `verify:visual` passes again: every scene except `starter` (red on
purpose, see below) now matches a golden that a fresh-context critic judged first. Before
this milestone, 63 of 70 scenes were red at clean HEAD, so the gate said nothing about any
change. Two causes:
- the goldens had not been rewritten since M315 (2026-09-23), through the M316–M338 UI work
  and the M303 "Quiet instrument" restyle;
- the three M336–M338 goldens had been committed at 2x instead of `GOLDEN_SCALE` 0.5, so
  they could only ever fail with "the size changed".

This milestone also fixes the harness bug M336–M338 found and left:
`scripts/shot.cjs` passed its two run-ledger functions at the wrong positions. Built
2026-09-26 on local `main`.

## What landed

- **`shot.cjs` passes the run ledger where `registerIpcHandlers` reads it.** The two
  functions sat at positions 28/29 (`preview`, `assets`), so the preview and asset doors
  received functions, and `ledgerTimeline` / `ledgerEvent` (36/37) kept their empty
  defaults. Every Artifacts and Timeline shot from M300 to M338 therefore photographed an
  unwired record. The tail is now spelled `...Array(8)` (preview … publisher), the two
  ledger functions, then `...Array(5)` (checkOutput … tasks), with a comment naming each
  position range.
- **Every red scene was re-judged by a fresh-context critic, then re-written** with
  `UPDATE_GOLDENS=1`. That covers 62 scenes, including the three M336–M338 scenes at the
  correct scale. The critics were four Agents with no repo context. Each was handed only
  the fresh capture, the last golden, the pixel diff, the scene's intent (extracted from
  `shot.cjs`) and `docs/product-rules.md`'s critic section. The four `orchestration*`
  critics also got the `.vs-reference.png` composites. Their sentences are below,
  verbatim.

## What the critics found, and what happens to it

Most DEFECT verdicts are **pre-existing**: the golden already shows the same thing, and
the critic says so. Those are recorded here and under Owed, and they are not blockers for
a baseline whose job is to make the NEXT change visible. The **new** defects — each a
regression since M315 that the stale goldens hid — are fixed in **M341**, and each
changed scene gets a critic's sentence there before its golden is rewritten:

**Declined, with the reasons written here so a later session does not undo them:**
- **flip**: "the `1 session running` pill covers the flipped review title". The pill is a
  floating HUD over the canvas, and the camera moved 37px when the navigator changed. A
  HUD that never overlaps canvas content would need its own reserved band. Recorded,
  not built.
- **board**: "empty bands above and below" the card title. Those bands are the card's
  contextual verbs (Focus, Show on canvas) held open at rest. Their space is reserved
  because the density rule (CLAUDE.md, *contextual*: opacity 0 → 1) forbids a reflow on
  hover.
- **relay**: "the terminal text has no left padding". This was declined in M338 by the
  terminal slot's rule (styles.css): padding breaks clicks at a zoomed-out scale.
- **orchestration***, "vs reference": the divergence lists (silhouette, material, glow)
  are the input to a future reference pass. This milestone changes no pixel of the
  scene. The Artifacts and Timeline tabs the critic asks about are real (Phase E) and are
  now wired in the harness by this milestone's fix.
