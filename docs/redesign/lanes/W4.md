## Goal

Picking Codex glides the camera in and turns on Follow. Breadcrumb `World › Ledger CSV export › Codex · Esc`. An inline ask chip with Approve ⌘Y. A 400px focus sheet with the diff, Approve / Deny / Full diff, what the agent is doing, facts, a reply composer, and Open in Canvas ⌘↵ / Sessions.

Files. `world/world-select.ts`, a new `world/WorldFocusSheet.tsx` (plain DOM, imports no three), `WorldCard.tsx` (the card body is in this file; there is no `WorldCardBody.tsx`), the breadcrumb in `WorldChrome.tsx`, and `WorldView.tsx` (camera glide and follow through W3's `useWorldCamera`).

- Click (or Enter on a keyboard-picked robot) glides the camera in, turns on Follow, and shows the breadcrumb. Esc steps back out to the room, and ⌘Esc goes back to Plan.
- The sheet is 400px wide and has three sections. REQUEST: the question, the diff, and Approve ⌘Y (filled while a request is pending) / Deny / Full diff. WHAT IT'S DOING: plan steps with states. Facts: model · context %, branch, spend vs cap (inspector density, allowed by D3). Below them, the reply composer and Open in Canvas ⌘↵ · Sessions. When nothing is pending, Open in Canvas becomes the filled control, so there is still one primary per surface.
- Approve, Deny and Reply call the same verbs as the 2D inspector and Sessions, through the palette executor. The reply is enabled for chat agents (`agent:send`) and agent terminals (paste + explicit Send). It is disabled with the reason "answer in its terminal" for plain shells.
- Open in Canvas (⌘↵ or double-click) runs the reverse transition, lands the 2D camera on the panel, and selects it (M429 behaviour kept).
